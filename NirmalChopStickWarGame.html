🥢 How to Play

  • ☝️
    Each player starts with 1 finger on each hand.
  • ⚔️
    On your turn, tap your hand then tap an opponent's hand to attack.
  • ➕
    Your fingers are added to theirs. e.g. 3 + 4 = 7
  • 💀
    Result of exactly 5 → that hand dies (0).
  • 🔄
    Result over 5 → subtract 5. 7→2, 9→4
  • ✂️
    Split rule: If one hand has an even number and the other is dead (0), you may split evenly between both hands instead of attacking. e.g. 4→0 splits to 2+2.
  • 🚫
    Dead hands (0) cannot attack.
  • 🏆
    Win by making both opponent hands dead!
  • 🎨
    Tap your avatar/name to customise your player!
